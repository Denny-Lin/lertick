# Screening log (schbench, hackbench)

Screening uses `eval.sh`: 15 s schbench runs after a 3 s warm-up, both hackbench modes and 5 repetitions per arm, with the arm order shuffled. `eval_judge.py eval/<round> <incumbent>` prints each metric's ratio to EEVDF and to the incumbent; below 1 is better, and RPS is inverted. Screening only finds candidates. A replacement is confirmed with 30 s runs and 10 repetitions before it counts.

## Incumbent after r10

The candidate is the clock fix, cgroup weights, a wakeup-only 2 ms sleeper credit, fair-order continuation, cache-affine pick (2 ms window, depth 8) and sync-wakeup queuing (2 ms window), in online mode. The rejected switches (wakeup preemption, deadline ordering, AI-gated sync shortcut) have been removed from the code. Confirmation: `SBTIME=30 SBWARM=5 eval.sh confirm 10 default main-on=base:online final-on=final:online final-fx=final:fixed`.
